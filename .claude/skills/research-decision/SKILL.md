# Research Decision

## Steps

1. State the question and constraints.
2. Prefer official or primary sources.
3. Compare options.
4. Recommend a decision.
5. Draft an ADR when needed.

## Outputs

- Research note.
- Sources.
- Recommendation.
- ADR draft if applicable.

## Failure Conditions

Uncited external claim, outdated source, or major stack change without ADR and human escalation.
